Parking authorization

The official map classifies Lots 13W, 13E, 14, 15 and 16 as permit parking, while Lot 8 is reserved. Lots 13W, 13E, and 14 are open, ungated campus parking areas and are physically accessible from public campus roads; however, their open access should not be interpreted as authorization to park. Please use these permit-designated lots only when SSCET event parking authorization, a pass, or on-site signage indicates that conference parking is permitted. If there is any uncertainty, the parking deck or metered visitor spaces are the preferred options.
